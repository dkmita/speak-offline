### Unit 6.30 — Get things fixed; Form the past subjunctive 🧠 inferred ⭐⭐⭐ MAJOR GRAMMAR PIVOT
- **Grammar:** **Past subjunctive (imperfect subjunctive) introduced.**
  - Formation: 3rd-person plural preterite, drop -ron, add `-ra/-ras/-ra/-ramos/-ran` (or alternate `-se/-ses/-se/-semos/-sen`)
  - **-ar**: hablar → hablaron → `hablara/hablaras/hablara/habláramos/hablaran`
  - **-er/-ir**: comer → comieron → `comiera`; vivir → vivieron → `viviera`
  - **Irregular preterites carry over**: `tener → tuviera`, `hacer → hiciera`, `ser/ir → fuera`, `estar → estuviera`, `decir → dijera`, `traer → trajera`
  - Use cases: past triggers requiring subjunctive (`Quería que viniera`), polite/softened requests (`Quisiera + inf`, `Si pudiera...`), hypothetical "If I were..." (`Si fuera...`)
- **Vocab:** All past subjunctive forms; fix-it context: `arreglar`, `reparar`, `el taller`, `el mecánico`, `el fontanero`/`el plomero`, `el electricista`, `funcionar`, `estropearse`, `descomponerse`
- **Samples:**
  - "Quería que viniera el plomero ayer." → I wanted the plumber to come yesterday.
  - "Si tuviera más dinero, compraría una casa." → If I had more money, I'd buy a house.
  - "Quisiera que me ayudaras." → I'd like you to help me.

### Unit 6.38 — Say how things happened; Use passive voice 🧠 inferred ⭐ grammar pivot
- **Grammar:** **Passive voice.**

  - **Impersonal `se`** (already from 4.45): "Se dice que...", "Se cree que..."
- **Vocab:** Passive constructions; `por` (by, agent), `según` (according to)
- **Samples:**

  - "Se venden manzanas frescas." → Fresh apples are sold.
  - "Se dice que va a llover." → It's said that it's going to rain.
