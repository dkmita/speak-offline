### Unit 4.20 — Use the imperfect tense; Form the past tense 🧠 inferred ⭐⭐ MAJOR GRAMMAR PIVOT
- **Grammar:** **Preterite vs. imperfect distinction taught explicitly.** This is the canonical "when to use which" unit.
  - **Preterite**: completed actions, specific time, sequential events ("Ayer fui al cine.")
  - **Imperfect**: ongoing/habitual past, descriptions, background, age, weather, time-of-day ("Llovía cuando salí.")
  - Combined narration: imperfect = setting, preterite = event.
- **Vocab:** Time markers signaling each tense; trigger words like `mientras`, `cuando`, `de repente`
- **Samples:**
  - "Llovía cuando salí de la casa." → It was raining when I left the house.
  - "Cada verano íbamos a la playa, pero el año pasado fuimos a la montaña." → Every summer we used to go to the beach, but last year we went to the mountains.
  - "Mientras estudiaba, sonó el teléfono." → While I was studying, the phone rang.

### Unit 4.34 — Talk about changes; Use the present perfect 🧠 inferred ⭐⭐ MAJOR GRAMMAR PIVOT
- **Grammar:** **Present perfect introduced.**
  - Auxiliary: `he`, `has`, `ha`, `hemos`, `han`
  - Past participles: -ar → -ado (hablado, trabajado), -er/-ir → -ido (comido, vivido)
  - **Irregular participles**: `hacer → hecho`, `decir → dicho`, `ver → visto`, `escribir → escrito`, `abrir → abierto`, `romper → roto`, `poner → puesto`, `volver → vuelto`, `morir → muerto`, `descubrir → descubierto`
  - Use: experiences ("Have you ever...?"), recent past, results that persist
  - Often paired with `ya`, `todavía no`, `nunca`, `alguna vez`, `recientemente`
- **Vocab:** Past participles above; `cambiar`, `el cambio`, `convertirse en`, `transformarse`
- **Samples:**
  - "He visitado España tres veces." → I have visited Spain three times.
  - "¿Has visto la nueva película?" → Have you seen the new movie?
  - "Todavía no hemos terminado." → We haven't finished yet.
  - "Mi vida ha cambiado mucho." → My life has changed a lot.

### Unit 4.37 — Use the future tense 🧠 inferred ⭐⭐ MAJOR GRAMMAR PIVOT

- **Grammar:** Revisits 3.7, 2.22, 2.15. Gustar-family verbs in multiple tenses.
- **Vocab:** `gustar`, `encantar`, `interesar`, `importar`, `fascinar`, `molestar`, `aburrir`
- **Samples:**
  - "Me encantó la película de anoche." → I loved the movie last night.

### Unit 4.51 — Make polite requests 🧠 inferred
- **Grammar:** **Conditional tense preview** for politeness:
  - `¿Podrías + inf?` (Could you...?)
  - `Me gustaría + inf` (I would like to...)
  - `¿Te importaría + inf?` (Would you mind...?)
  - `Querría` (I would want) — softer than `quiero`
  - Note: full conditional tense is taught in Section 5 (5.16); here it's introduced as fixed polite forms.
- **Vocab:** `por favor`, `¿podrías?`, `me gustaría`, `quisiera`, `¿te importaría?`, `¿sería posible?`
- **Samples:**
  - "¿Podrías abrir la ventana, por favor?" → Could you open the window, please?
  - "Me gustaría reservar una mesa." → I'd like to reserve a table.

### Unit 4.52 — Describe events 🧠 inferred
- **Grammar:** Preterite + imperfect combined for event narration (revisits 4.20, 4.40).
- **Vocab:** Event vocab; sequence connectors; descriptive vocab
- **Samples:**
  - "Fue una boda preciosa. Todos se divertían y bailaban toda la noche." → It was a beautiful wedding. Everyone was having fun and dancing all night.
